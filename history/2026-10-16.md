### The guard, and how the rule was arrived at

Section 41 requires every slash-qualified backticked path citation in the
project-owned doc set to resolve on disk. Getting a rule with zero false
positives took three passes over the real corpus, not a guess:

- **Naive** (first backticked word containing a `/`): 148 unresolving
  candidates across ten agent-facing docs. Noise from `path:line` references,
  placeholders in `<>`, `CI/CD`, `y/N`, `2>/dev/null`, `atalby/sec-cli`,
  `git@...`, and glob-shaped directories.
- **Rule v2** (strip a trailing `:N`/`:N-M`/`:N,M`; reject `://`, `*?<>@`;
  require a leading `[A-Za-z0-9._-]`; require a `/`; require the first segment
  to be a live top-level entry from `git ls-files`; require the full path to
  not exist): 8 findings. All 8 were read by hand and are legitimate --
  `.gemini/skills/` and `.claude/skills/` quoted as migrated-legacy paths in
  ADAPTERS.md, three hub-side `scripts/*.sh` and `scripts/*.py` paths (this
  repo's `scripts/` contains only `tests/`), and two deliberately-narrative
  profile.md sentences about paths a past issue had removed.
- **Rule v3** adds the payload exclusion: a candidate the payload itself cites
  (walking AGENTS.md and `methodology/*.md` with the same token regex) is
  hub-relative, not a local defect. That drops the payload's own
  `docs/hyer/specs/...` style citations and leaves exactly one finding,
  `docs/wiki`.

Scope is declared partial in the section header, as it must be: the doc set is
README.md, CLAUDE.md, every `docs/*.md` (globbed, so a new one is covered
without touching the guard), and `skills/architecture-360-audit/SKILL.md`. NOT
covered are ADAPTERS.md and profile.md, whose slash-qualified citations are
mostly hub-side or narrative -- a clean result there is not approval.

Two bugs I introduced while writing the rule, both caught by the corpus rather
than by reading the code:

- The reject set was written `"://*?<>@"`, which contains the `/` character
  twice. Since `/` is the separator the rule is trying to keep, every
  candidate was rejected and the section reported zero findings -- a guard that
  silently passes everything. Corrected to `":*?<>@"`.
- Whitespace-bearing tokens slipped through as paths. `docs/MULTI_TENANCY.md`
  cites `bin/bw-session-keeper rotate` and SKILL.md cites a
  `python3 ~/sandbox/hyer/scripts/...` invocation; both are commands, not
  paths. A backticked token containing whitespace is now rejected.
