- **#137** (`fix(sec)`, dispatch 86 -> 87) — the lane's first real *product*

  on bash < 4.4 — stock macOS, where `env bash` is 3.2 — plain `sec get KEY`

  the guarded inner form). RED note: the first scanner matched nothing because
  its name-extraction used `%%[*` — an unterminated bracket glob, which bash
  treats literally — and so failed green until rewritten with a sed
  extraction. A repo-wide sweep for the rest of the class (declare -A, mapfile,
  `${x,,}`, `&>>`, `[[ -v ]]`, and GNU-only grep/sed/readlink/sha256sum/xargs
  flags) found zero other live instances across every shell surface.
