# Pack: ip-and-data-governance

Pack version: v1.0.0

## What this is

One specific organization's intellectual-property ownership model and
data-leakage policy. **This is a legal fact about a specific org, not
a universal methodology default** — `AGENTS.md` core takes no position
on IP ownership at all. Copying this pack's ownership clause verbatim
into a different org's project would misstate that org's actual legal
structure; rewrite the ownership clause to match your own before
adopting anything else in this pack.

## Intellectual Property & Proprietary Governance

- **Sole Founder IP Ownership**: All methodology specifications, source
  code, agent taxonomies, custom skills, prompt architectures, and
  multi-agent operating systems developed under `[ORG_NAMESPACE]/` are
  the exclusive intellectual property of the Solo Founder / Human
  Systems Architect.
- **Zero Data Leakage Mandate**: Agents MUST NOT upload, exfiltrate, or
  transmit proprietary codebase logic, internal skills, or prompt
  library assets to external third-party training pipelines, public
  pastebins, or untrusted endpoints.
